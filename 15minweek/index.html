15 Min Week

Can you make it through one week?

Every 15 minutes, write one line about what you just did. Stick with it for a single week and this will be your most productive week ever.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

⏰

Set your work day

Pick your start and end times and your work days. Monday to Friday by default.

🔔

Get a nudge every 15 minutes

Choose a beep, an alarm or a silent buzz. Tap anywhere to silence it.

✍️

Write one line

"What did you do for the last 15 minutes?" Answer right from the notification, or in the app.

📊

See your day

A summary every evening: where your time went and a clean timeline of your day.

🎉

Finish the week

Make it to Friday and celebrate with confetti. Then decide if you're up for another round.

A look inside

🔒
Private by design

Your answers are stored only on your iPhone. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy.
